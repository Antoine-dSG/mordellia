Coverage

Chapter I comprises twelve numbered articles, pp. 1–23. All are included in the connected mathematical treatment. The digital edition has seven readings and 28 selected Original passages, with matching Modernized excerpts. It is not a complete transcription or a complete modern proof of Rohn’s classification.

The full Preface, Contents, Addenda, Introduction and Chapter I were read for the initial synthesis. Published excerpts were visually collated against scans; the full Introduction was used for context, not independently verified in all of its later-chapter mathematics.
