Dickson Volume I: coverage

14 October 2026. Source: History of the Theory of Numbers, Volume I: Divisibility and Primality, Carnegie Institution of Washington, 1919.

Read: the title and publication leaves, Preface iii–xii, contents xiii, and Chapter I pp. 3–50. The Preface supplies the introduction; there is no separate introductory chapter. The next chapter begins on p. 51 and is untreated.

Developed: a connected mathematical treatment of Chapter I's principal mechanisms, across six mathematical reading units and an opening guide. Original and Modernized are selected extracts, not full-chapter editions. The website states this on its homepage and each reading page. No raw OCR is presented as a transcription. Original v1 is frozen; its 28 selected passages have been checked against scans by the authoring assistant, without independent collation.

The six numbered reading units are editorial divisions, not sections numbered by Dickson. They preserve the order of the chapter's topics. Reinterpreted may reorganize arguments, notably using p. 30 in the earlier perfect-number unit. Named alignment groups record these relationships.
