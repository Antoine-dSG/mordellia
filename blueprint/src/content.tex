
\section*{Conventions}

In the following list, we record the equations and congruences considered by Mordell,
grouped by degree and, from degree $2$ onward, by homogeneity. Within each group,
entries are ordered by their LEC labels from the List of Equations and Congruences in
\cite[pp.~307--312]{mordell-diophantine-equations}.

Page references are Mordell's printed page numbers.

Mathematical notation has been normalized and modernised, when deemed necessary.

All mistakes and omissions are my responsibility. Please consult the printed edition in case of doubt.
