\section*{Conventions}

In the following sections, we list the equations and congruences considered by Mordell.

The labels \textbf{EQ-001}, \textbf{EQ-002}, \ldots index equations in order of appearance in Mordell's book (and order of appearance in this document).

The labels \textbf{LEC-001} through \textbf{LEC-182} index equations in order of appearance in Mordell's List of Equations and Congruences (pp.~307--312).
The labels \textbf{LEC-183} and beyond record further equations of interest from the body text.

Page references are Mordell's printed page numbers.

Mathematical notation has been normalized and modernised, when deemed necessary.

All mistakes and omissions are my responsibility. Please consult the printed edition in case of doubt.
