\begin{proposition}
    Let $K$ be an algebraic number field of degree $n>3$, and let $\alpha,\beta,\gamma\in K$ be linearly independent over $\mathbb{Q}$ with $K=\mathbb{Q}(\alpha/\gamma,\beta/\gamma)$. If $\alpha x+\beta y+\gamma z$ is not of the form $\lambda R+\mu S$, where $R$ is a rational form and $S$ is a real form whose coefficients are in a quadratic field, and if $f(x,y,z)$ has degree $<n/4$, then
\[
    N(\alpha x+\beta y+\gamma z)=f(x,y,z)
\]
    has only finitely many integer solutions.
\end{proposition}
