\begin{proposition}
Suppose that a rational solution $(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ of
\[
    y^2=a_1x^2+b_1x+c_1,\qquad z^2=a_2x^2+b_2x+c_2
\]
is known. On putting $x=x_1+X$, one may take $x_1=0$ and replace
$c_1,c_2$ by squares. In the first equation, put
\[
    y=tx+c_1.
\]
Then
\[
    x=\frac{b_1-2c_1t}{t^2-a_1}.
\]
\end{proposition}
