\begin{proposition}
    From a known rational solution of
\[
    \begin{aligned}y^2(ax^2+bx+c)&+y(a_1x^2+b_1x+c_1)\\&\qquad+a_2x^2+b_2x+c_2=0\end{aligned}
\]
    one obtains in general an infinite sequence of rational solutions by alternately solving the equation as a quadratic in $y$ and in $x$.
\end{proposition}
