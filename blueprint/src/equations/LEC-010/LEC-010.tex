\begin{proposition}
Let
\[
    f(\mathbf{x})=a_1x_1^2+a_2x_2^2+a_3x_3^2+a_4x_4^2.
\]
The equation $f(\mathbf{x})=0$ is solvable in rational integers if and only if
$f(\mathbf{x})\equiv0\pmod {p^r}$ is solvable for all primes $p$ and all
$r\ge1$ with $(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4,p)=1$, and $f(\mathbf{x})=0$ is solvable in
real numbers.
\end{proposition}
