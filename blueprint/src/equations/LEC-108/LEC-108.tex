\begin{proposition}
    The integer solutions of
\[
    z^2=x^3+y^3
\]
    with $y$ odd and prime to $x$ are given by
\[
    \begin{array}{ll}
    x=-4p^3q+4q^4, & y=p^4+8pq^3,\\
    x=-p^4+6p^2q^2+3q^4, & y=p^4+6p^2q^2-3q^4,\\
    x=p^4+6p^2q^2-3q^4, & y=-p^4+6p^2q^2+3q^4,\\
    x=2p^4-4p^3q-4pq^3+2q^4, & y=p^4+4p^3q-6p^2q^2+4pq^3+q^4,\\
    x=4p^3q+24p^2q^2+48pq^3+36q^4, & y=p^4+8p^3q+24p^2q^2+24pq^3.
    \end{array}
\]
    Here $p,q$ take integer values such that $y$ is odd and prime to $x$.
\end{proposition}
