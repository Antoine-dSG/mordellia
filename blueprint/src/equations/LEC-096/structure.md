# Structure

See `research/clusters/markoff-mutation/structure.md`.

The determining structure is the Vieta mutation group. Fixing two variables, the equation is quadratic in the third, and the complementary-root operation preserves integrality:
\[
    x\mapsto ayz-x,\qquad y\mapsto azx-y,\qquad z\mapsto axy-z.
\]
