# History

See `research/clusters/mordell-elliptic/history.md`.

`LEC-066` is the Mordell curve family. Mordell traces it through Bachet's tangent method, Fermat's integer problem for \(k=-2\), Fueter's rational infinitude theorem, Thue/binary-cubic finiteness for integer points, and Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer computations of ranks.
