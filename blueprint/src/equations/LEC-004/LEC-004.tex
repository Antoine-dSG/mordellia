\begin{proposition}
If one integer solution not $(0,0,0)$ of the irreducible equation
\[
    ax^2+by^2+cz^2+2fyz+2gzx+2hxy=0
\]
exists, then the general solution with $(x,y,z)=1$ is given by a finite number of
expressions of the form
\[
    x=A_1p^2+B_1pq+C_1q^2,\quad
    y=A_2p^2+B_2pq+C_2q^2,\quad
    z=A_3p^2+B_3pq+C_3q^2,
\]
where $p,q$ take all integer values with $(p,q)=1$, and the $A,B,C$ are integer
constants.
\end{proposition}
