\begin{proposition}
    In general, the equation
\[
    ax^3+by^3+cz^3+d=0
\]
    where $abcd\ne0$, has no solutions with $x,y,z$ relatively prime polynomials with rational coefficients of degree at most $4$ in a parameter, except for
\[
    x^3+y^3+cz^3+c=0,\qquad x^3+y^3+cz^3+2=0,\qquad x^3+y^3+2z^3+2=0,
\]
    where $c\ne2r^3$ for rational $r$.
\end{proposition}
