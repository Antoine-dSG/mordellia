# Literature

## Modern Formulation

Louboutin and Newman prove the exact criterion for absence of positive
solutions in terms of the ideal class group of
\(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})\). Under the generalized Riemann hypothesis this gives
exactly the eighteen values
\[
  1,2,4,6,10,18,22,30,42,58,70,78,102,130,190,210,330,462.
\]

Kani records two further formulations. An integer \(d\) is idoneal exactly
when \(xy+yz+zx=d\) has no solution in distinct positive integers; and
idoneality is equivalent to the ring class group of discriminant \(-4d\)
being an elementary abelian \(2\)-group. It is also equivalent to the
principal binary theta series being Eisenstein, or to the associated
theta-series span containing no nonzero cusp form.

## Relation To Mordell

- Mordell's reduced-form classes = elements of the discriminant-\(-4d\)
  class group.
- Boundary-only representations = no strictly positive solution.
- Idoneality = every class is its own inverse.
- Exceptional \(d\) = class group of exponent at most \(2\), with the three
  elementary exceptions stated separately.
