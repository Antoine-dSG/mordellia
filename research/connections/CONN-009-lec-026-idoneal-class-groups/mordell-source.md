# Mordell Source

## Location

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, pp. 291--292.
- LEC tag: `LEC-026`.

## Atom

Mordell counts nonnegative solutions of
\[
    xy+yz+zx=d
\]
through reduced binary quadratic forms.  His discussion includes boundary
solutions such as permutations of \((1,0,d)\) and uses class-number-one
information to constrain when additional solutions can occur.

The modern theorem changes the boundary condition from nonnegative to
strictly positive variables.  Under Mordell's correspondence, a positive
solution corresponds to a reduced form outside the boundary classes.  The
failure of such a form to exist is exactly the exponent-\(2\) class-group
condition recorded in `CONN-009`.

## Factorization Coordinate

For fixed \(z\),
\[
    (x+z)(y+z)=d+z^2.
\]
Thus strict-positive solutions are nontrivial factorizations of \(d+z^2\)
subject to \(x,y>0\). Euler's and Grube's idoneal criteria impose precisely
the factorization rigidity that prevents such solutions.
