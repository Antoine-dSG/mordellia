# Mordell Source

## Location

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, pp. 291--292.
- Primary LEC tag: `LEC-026`.
- Related LEC tag: `LEC-024`, through its three-square specialization.

## Atoms

Mordell associates a solution of
\[
    xy+yz+zx=d,
    \qquad x,y,z\geq0,
\]
to a reduced binary quadratic form \([A,B,C]\) of determinant \(-d\) by
\[
    A=x+y,
    \qquad |B|=x,
    \qquad C=x+z.
\]
He counts forms equivalent to \([a,0,a]\) and \([a,a,a]\) with weights
\(1/2\) and \(1/3\), and counts solutions with \(xyz=0\) with weight \(1/2\).
His Theorem 2 states that the resulting solution count is \(3G(d)\).

Mordell's `LEC-024` material includes the theorem that
\[
    x^2+y^2+z^2=m
\]
is soluble for positive \(m\) exactly when \(m\) is not of the form
\(4^a(8b+7)\). Its exact representation numbers are also governed by
Hurwitz class numbers.

## Normalization Check

Mordell writes forms as \(AX^2+2BXY+CY^2\). Determinant \(-d\) means
\(B^2-AC=-d\), so the standard discriminant is
\[
    (2B)^2-4AC=-4d.
\]
His special weights agree with the standard definition of \(H(4d)\).
