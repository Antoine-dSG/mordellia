# Literature

## Modern Formulation

The Hurwitz class number \(H(N)\) is the weighted number of positive binary
quadratic-form classes of discriminant \(-N\), with weights \(1/2\) and \(1/3\)
for the two classes with extra automorphisms.  Zagier proved that its generating
series admits a non-holomorphic completion of weight \(3/2\); in modern
language its holomorphic part is a mock modular form with unary theta shadow.

Gauss's three-square class-number formula expresses \(r_3(n)\) in terms of the
same \(H(N)\). Consequently `LEC-026` and the three-square case of `LEC-024`
are not merely both quadratic-form problems: their representation counts are
Fourier coefficients of the same class-number series.

## Relation To Mordell

- Mordell's \(G(d)\) = the Hurwitz class number \(H(4d)\).
- Mordell's weights = automorphism weights in \(H(4d)\).
- Mordell's exact count \(3G(d)\) = \(3H(4d)\).
- Three-square representation numbers = a second arithmetic interpretation
  of the same class-number coefficients.
