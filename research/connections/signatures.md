# Structural Signatures

Use these signatures to search from modern structure back toward Mordell's
methods.

- `complementary_root_involution`: replace one root of a polynomial equation
  by the other root while keeping the other variables fixed.
- `exchange_relation`: rewrite a Diophantine equation as
  \(x_{\mathrm{old}}x_{\mathrm{new}}=P(\text{other variables})\).
- `laurent_phenomenon`: a recurrence has Laurent polynomial iterates in the
  initial data, giving systematic integrality after specialization.
- `mutation_graph`: solutions are organized by repeated local mutations rather
  than by independent parametrisations.
- `character_variety`: the equation is an affine trace/character variety or a
  close specialization.
- `coxeter_weyl_reflection`: transformations behave like reflections in a root
  system or Weyl group.
- `continued_fraction_unit_recurrence`: solutions are generated by units or
  continued fractions.
- `norm_form_factorisation`: the arithmetic is controlled by a norm map in a
  number field or algebraic torus.
- `torsor_descent`: Mordell's manipulation is naturally a torsor or descent
  calculation.
- `local_global_obstruction`: a local obstruction controls solubility.
- `brauer_manin_candidate`: a local-global failure suggests a Brauer-Manin
  explanation.
- `brauer_manin_obstruction`: a Brauer class and its local invariants explain
  a failure of an integral local-global principle or strong approximation.
- `strong_approximation_failure`: integral points omit adelically permitted
  congruence classes for a structural reason.
- `cayley_cubic`: after homogenisation or rescaling, the equation is the
  four-nodal Cayley cubic surface.
- `log_k3_surface`: the affine equation is naturally a log K3 surface, so its
  integral points are governed by the geometry of an open surface.
- `universal_torsor`: coprime factor coordinates form, or are charts on, a
  universal torsor over the underlying variety.
- `tropical_valuation_dynamics`: valuations or degree vectors follow a
  piecewise-linear mutation or reduction dynamic.
- `variable_modulus_divisibility`: an unknown occurs in the modulus, so
  integrality is equivalent to divisibility by a product of coordinates.
- `factorization_coordinate`: the equation is equivalent to a factorization
  such as \((x+z)(y+z)=d+z^2\), and factor rigidity controls solubility.
- `hurwitz_class_number`: weighted solution counts are Hurwitz class numbers.
- `half_integral_weight_modularity`: representation counts are Fourier
  coefficients of a modular, mock modular, or harmonic Maass form of
  half-integral weight.
- `representation_number`: the atom records an exact arithmetic count rather
  than only existence or parametrisation.
- `idoneal_discriminant`: exceptional solubility is equivalent to an idoneal
  quadratic discriminant.
- `elementary_2_class_group`: the qualitative answer is controlled by a class
  group in which every element has order at most two.
- `affine_cubic_surface`: the divisibility equation is the integral-point
  problem on an affine cubic surface.
- `geiser_involution`: a recurrence is induced by a Geiser-type involution on
  a Del Pezzo model.
- `infinite_discrete_automorphism_group`: infinitely many solutions arise as
  an orbit of an infinite-order surface automorphism.
- `companion_surface_groupoid`: elementary transformations move among
  companion integral models and compose to an automorphism of the original
  surface.
