# Claim

## Precise Claim

Mordell's positive-solution method for
\[
    x^2+y^2+1=xyz
\]
is the \((m,n)=(2,2)\) extremal case of the Mordell--Schinzel
surface/cluster-frieze correspondence. In the notation of the MS surface
manuscript,
\[
    Y_{2,2}=(xyz=x^2+y^2+1).
\]
Its positive integer points correspond to friezes of the rank-two cluster
algebra with mutation matrix
\[
    \begin{pmatrix}0&2\\-2&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For positive integer solutions, Mordell's reduction gives \(z=3\) and
consecutive terms in the sequence
\[
    1,1,2,5,13,\ldots.
\]
This is the specialization at \(X_0=X_1=1\) of the coefficient-free rank-two
cluster recurrence
\[
    X_{n-1}X_{n+1}=X_n^2+1.
\]

The exchange matrix has \(mn=4\), hence is the tame/infinite boundary case.
The finite/infinite classification for positive points on extremal MS surfaces
matches the finite/infinite classification of rank-two cluster algebras:
\[
    Y_{m,n}(\mathbb Z_{>0}) \text{ is finite}
    \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
    mn\le 3.
\]
Thus the infinitude of `LEC-117` is explained by the infinite cluster type of
the associated rank-two seed.
