# Mordell Source

## Location

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, pp. 106--110.
- Main LEC tag: `LEC-096`.
- Nearby LEC tags: `LEC-094`, `LEC-098`.

## Atom

Mordell studies
\[
    x^2+y^2+z^2-axyz=b
\]
as a quadratic equation in one variable with the other two fixed. Replacing a
coordinate by the other root preserves the equation.

For example, fixing \(y,z\), the two \(x\)-roots have sum \(ayz\), so the
operation is
\[
    x\longmapsto ayz-x.
\]

## Normal Forms

- \(x^2+y^2+z^2-axyz=b\).
- After scaling when \(a\ne0\):
  \[
      X^2+Y^2+Z^2-XYZ=a^2b.
  \]
- Markoff-Hurwitz polynomial:
  \[
      H_n=x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2-x_1\cdots x_n.
  \]
