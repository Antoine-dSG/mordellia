# Literature

## Modern Formulation

Markoff-Hurwitz equations are preserved by Vieta involutions. The automorphism
group generated by these transformations is studied as a structural object in
modern work. For classical Markoff surfaces, the same equation also appears as
a trace/character-variety equation.

## Relation To Mordell

This is not a highly distant discovery for `LEC-096`, since Mordell explicitly
places the equation near Markoff and Hurwitz. Its value is as an anchor:
the same complementary-root operation later reappears in the asymmetric
`LEC-117` and `LEC-118` recurrences, where the cluster interpretation is much
less visible from Mordell's language.
