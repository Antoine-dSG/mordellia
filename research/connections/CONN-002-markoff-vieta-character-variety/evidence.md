## Proof Sketch

Fix \(y,z\). The equation
\[
    x^2-ayzx+(y^2+z^2-b)=0
\]
has roots \(x\) and \(x'\) satisfying
\[
    x+x'=ayz.
\]
Thus
\[
    x'=ayz-x
\]
is an involution preserving the equation. The same calculation applies to the
other variables. These are exactly the complementary-root/Vieta moves used in
the Markoff-Hurwitz literature.
