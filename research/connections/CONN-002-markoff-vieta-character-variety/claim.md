# Claim

## Precise Claim

Mordell's elementary operations for the symmetric Markoff-type equation
\[
    x^2+y^2+z^2-axyz=b
\]
are Vieta mutations. In the normalized Markoff-Hurwitz setting these are the
standard complementary-root automorphisms, and in the classical trace setting
they are related to automorphisms of an affine character variety.

## Qualitative Behavior Explained

The operation generates solution orbits from fundamental solutions. This is a
structural anchor for comparing symmetric Markoff equations with the asymmetric
cluster branch in `CONN-001`.
