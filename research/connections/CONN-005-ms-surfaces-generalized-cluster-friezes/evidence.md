## Theorem-Level Evidence

The local manuscript proves:

- universal separated MS families admit rank-two generalized cluster
  structures;
- positive integer points on a fiber correspond to adapted friezes;
- for \(Y_{m,n}=(xyz=x^m+y^n+1)\), positive integer points are finite if and
  only if \(mn\le3\).

For `LEC-117`, \(m=n=2\), so \(mn=4\). This puts Mordell's equation exactly in
the infinite affine/tame case.

## Failure Modes

The statement applies directly to positive separated non-degenerate surfaces.
It does not automatically cover arbitrary signs or arbitrary non-separated
polynomials in Mordell's miscellaneous section.
