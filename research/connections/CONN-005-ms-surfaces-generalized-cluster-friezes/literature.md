# Literature

## Modern Formulation

The local manuscript `Positive integer points on Mordell--Schinzel surfaces`
constructs a universal family
\[
    \mathcal M_{m,n}=(XYZ=A(X)+B(Y)+\kappa)
\]
and a rank-two generalized cluster seed with mutation matrix
\[
    \begin{pmatrix}0&m\\-n&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The coordinate ring of the universal family is identified with the associated
generalized upper cluster algebra, and positive integer points on a fiber are
identified with adapted friezes.

## Relation To Mordell

Mordell's page-293 exchange equation is exactly the shape of a separated
Mordell--Schinzel surface. The manuscript supplies the modern structure that
Mordell did not have: generalized cluster mutation, upper cluster algebra, and
friezes.
