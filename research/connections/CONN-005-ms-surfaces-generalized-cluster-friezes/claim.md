# Claim

## Precise Claim

Positive Mordell--Schinzel surfaces
\[
    Y_G=(xyz=G(x,y))
\]
with separated positive polynomial
\[
    G(x,y)=A(x)+B(y)+\kappa
\]
are governed by rank-two generalized cluster algebras. Positive integer
points on a fiber correspond to adapted friezes of the associated generalized
upper cluster algebra.

For the extremal subfamily
\[
    Y_{m,n}=(xyz=x^m+y^n+1),
\]
the finiteness criterion is
\[
    Y_{m,n}(\mathbb Z_{>0})\text{ is finite}
    \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
    mn\le3.
\]

Thus `LEC-117` is the \((m,n)=(2,2)\) infinite/tame case.

## Modern Object

## Qualitative Behavior Explained

The finite/infinite behavior of positive integer points is controlled by the
rank-two cluster type. This is the structural reason behind the infinitude of
the `LEC-117` branch.
