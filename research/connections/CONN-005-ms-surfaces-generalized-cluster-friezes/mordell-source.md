## Atom

Mordell's equation
\[
    g(x)+h(y)+c=zxy
\]
is a separated Mordell--Schinzel surface equation. The positive-sign examples
\[
    x^2+y^2+1=xyz,\qquad
    x^2+y^2+x+y+1=xyz
\]
are positive non-degenerate fibers of this separated family.

## Normal Forms

- \(xyz=A(x)+B(y)+\kappa\).
- Extremal fiber: \(xyz=x^m+y^n+1\).
- Calibration fiber: \(xyz=x^2+y^2+1\).
