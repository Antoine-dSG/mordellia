# Claim

## Precise Claim

Let
\[
    U_n:\quad 4xyz=n(xy+xz+yz).
\]
Bright and Loughran prove that \(U_n\) is a singular log K3 surface and
\[
    \operatorname{Br}U_n/\operatorname{Br}\mathbb Q
    \cong \mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,
\]
generated by the transcendental quaternion class
\[
    \alpha=\left(-\frac{x}{z},-\frac{y}{z}\right).
\]
For odd \(n\), every positive integral solution satisfies
\[
    \prod_{p\mid n}
    \left(-\frac{x}{z},-\frac{y}{z}\right)_p=-1,
\]
where \((\ ,\ )_p\) is the Hilbert symbol.  For \(n=p\) prime this gives a
Legendre-symbol non-residue condition on a suitable ratio of two
denominators.  Bright--Loughran show that these local evaluations recover and
unify quadratic-reciprocity conditions recorded by Mordell from Yamamoto.

There is no Brauer--Manin obstruction to the existence of positive integral
solutions, so this structure does not settle the Erdos--Straus conjecture.
There is, however, always a Brauer--Manin obstruction to strong approximation
in the positive component.  The Brauer class therefore determines genuine
arithmetic restrictions on how solutions are distributed.

## Modern Object

A transcendental Brauer class on a singular log K3 surface and the resulting
Brauer--Manin obstruction to strong approximation.

## Qualitative Behavior Explained

The global Brauer class explains why apparently separate quadratic-character
conditions occur and why positive solutions occupy only restricted adelic
classes.  The same obstruction framework applies to the unlike-looking
Markoff surfaces contained in `LEC-096`.
