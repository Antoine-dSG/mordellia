# Mordell Source

## Location

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, pp. 287--290.
- Primary LEC tag: `LEC-188`.
- Related LEC tag: `LEC-096`.

## Atoms

After dividing prime-denominator solutions into the cases where one or two
denominators are divisible by \(p\), Mordell records Yamamoto's use of the
Legendre--Jacobi and Kronecker characters.  The resulting conditions include
quadratic-character restrictions obtained from congruences such as
\[
    p\equiv-\frac ba\pmod q,
    \qquad q\equiv-1\pmod{4ab}.
\]

Mordell presents these as reciprocity calculations attached to individual
parametrisations.  The modern connection packages them as local evaluations
of one global Brauer class on the surface \(U_n\).

The related Mordell family
\[
    x^2+y^2+z^2-axyz=b
\]
in `LEC-096` contains, at \(a=1\), the Markoff surfaces
\[
    x^2+y^2+z^2-xyz=m.
\]
These are also affine cubic log K3 surfaces studied through the integral
Brauer--Manin obstruction.
