# Literature

## Modern Formulation

Bright and Loughran compute the Brauer group of the singular Erdos--Straus
surface and evaluate its non-constant class on adelic integral points.  Their
main consequences are:

- there is no Brauer--Manin obstruction to the existence of positive integral
  solutions;
- there is always a Brauer--Manin obstruction to strong approximation in the
  positive real component;
- for odd \(n\), a product of Hilbert symbols over primes dividing \(n\) must
  equal \(-1\);
- the prime case recovers Yamamoto's quadratic-reciprocity restrictions;
- positive and non-positive integral solutions have opposite real/Brauer
  signs.

The Markoff subfamily of `LEC-096` belongs to a parallel theory. Work of
Colliot-Thelene--Wei--Xu and Loughran--Mitankin studies integral Hasse
principles and strong approximation on
\[
    x^2+y^2+z^2-xyz=m
\]
through its Brauer group.

## Relation To Mordell

- Mordell/Yamamoto quadratic characters = local Hilbert-symbol evaluations of
  \(\alpha\).
- Separate reciprocity formulas = specialisations of a single global Brauer
  class.
- Positivity of \(x,y,z\) = choice of the positive connected component of
  \(U_n(\mathbb R)\), on which the real invariant is constant and non-trivial.
- Missing congruence classes of solutions = failure of strong approximation.
- `LEC-188` and the Markoff subfamily of `LEC-096` = distinct affine cubic log
  K3 surfaces governed by the same local-global mechanism.
