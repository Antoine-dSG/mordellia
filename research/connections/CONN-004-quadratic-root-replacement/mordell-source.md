# Mordell Source

## Location

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, pp. 296--299.
- Connected LEC tags: `LEC-117`, `LEC-118`, `LEC-183`.

## Atom

Mordell considers special quadratic polynomials \(f_1(x,y)\), \(f_2(x,y)\) and
an equation
\[
    z=\frac{f_1(x,y)}{f_2(x,y)}.
\]
For fixed \(z\), this becomes a quadratic equation in one variable. If one
integral root is known, the second root is integral under Mordell's divisibility
hypotheses. Repeating this operation gives a two-sided sequence.

## Normal Forms

- \(z f_2(x,y)=f_1(x,y)\).
- Complementary-root move:
  \[
      x\mapsto x'
  \]
  where \(x,x'\) are the two roots of the same quadratic in \(x\).
- Sequence form:
  \[
      \ldots,u_0,u_1,u_2,\ldots.
  \]
