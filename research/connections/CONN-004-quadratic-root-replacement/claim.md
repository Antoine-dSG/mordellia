# Claim

## Precise Claim

Mordell's method for quadratic rational equations linear in one unknown is a
general mutation engine: fix the value of the linear unknown, view the remaining
equation as a quadratic in one variable, and replace one integral root by the
other. This is the mechanism behind `LEC-117`, `LEC-118`, and the broader
exchange family around `LEC-183`.

## Mordell Atom

- Atom id: `ATOM-MISC-QUADRATIC-ROOT-REPLACEMENT`.
- Type: method.
- LEC tags: `LEC-117`, `LEC-118`, `LEC-183`.
- Mordell pages: pp. 296--299.

## Modern Object

Mutation by complementary root, with possible interpretations in cluster,
generalized cluster, or LP-algebra settings depending on the exchange
polynomial.

## Qualitative Behavior Explained

The method explains why solutions naturally organize into bi-infinite sequences
or finite cycles, rather than independent ad hoc solutions.
