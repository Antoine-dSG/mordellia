## Current Evidence

Mordell explicitly uses the other-root construction in the quadratic rational
equation discussion and then applies it to `LEC-117` and `LEC-118`. The
`LEC-117` specialization is already identified in `CONN-001` as a rank-two
cluster recurrence.

## Failure Modes

Root replacement alone is not enough to claim cluster algebra. A high-rank
connection requires a closed mutation rule, a Laurent or integrality theorem,
and an explanation of the qualitative arithmetic behavior.
