# Literature

## Modern Formulation

Complementary-root replacement is the algebraic core of many mutation
procedures. In cluster algebras it appears as exchange mutation; in
Markoff-Hurwitz equations it appears as a Vieta involution; in LP algebras it
can appear through non-binomial exchange polynomials.

## Relation To Mordell

This method-level connection is broader than a single equation. It should be
used to search the book for passages where Mordell generates solutions by
root replacement or recurrence, even when no obvious Markoff equation is
present.
