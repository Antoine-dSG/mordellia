# Literature

## Modern Formulation

The MS-surface manuscript constructs universal families
\[
    \mathcal M_{m,n}=(XYZ=A(X)+B(Y)+\kappa)
\]
and identifies their integral coordinate rings with generalized upper cluster
algebras of rank two. Positive integer points on a fiber correspond to adapted
friezes.

Ordinary cluster algebras are too narrow for the full polynomial family.
Generalized cluster algebras handle the separated positive subfamily directly.
Laurent phenomenon algebras may still be relevant for still broader variants of
Mordell's equation
\[
    g(x)+h(y)+c=zxy.
\]

## Relation To Mordell

Mordell's congruence condition is exactly the integrality condition for an
exchange value. For positive separated \(A(x)+B(y)+\kappa\), the manuscript
provides the missing structure: rank-two generalized cluster mutation and a
frieze interpretation of positive integer points.
