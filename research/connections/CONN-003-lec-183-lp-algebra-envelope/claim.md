# Claim

## Precise Claim

Mordell's general equation
\[
    g(x)+h(y)+c=zxy
\]
is an exchange-polynomial envelope for the asymmetric equations `LEC-117` and
`LEC-118`. For the separated positive subfamily
\[
    xyz=A(x)+B(y)+\kappa,
\]
the local manuscript `Positive integer points on Mordell--Schinzel surfaces`
gives a generalized rank-two cluster structure on the universal family.

Laurent phenomenon algebras remain a possible envelope for wider polynomial
families, but the first modern structure to use here is generalized cluster
algebra, not ordinary cluster algebra.

This is a candidate connection, not yet a promoted theorem.

## Modern Object

Generalized cluster algebra with exchange polynomial
\[
    P(x,y)=g(x)+h(y)+c.
\]

## Qualitative Behavior Explained

The generalized cluster structure explains Laurent positivity and the frieze
interpretation of positive integer points for the positive separated subfamily.
