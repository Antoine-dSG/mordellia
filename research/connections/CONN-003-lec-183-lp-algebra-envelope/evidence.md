## Current Evidence

The specialization \(g(x)=x^2\), \(h(y)=y^2\), \(c=1\) is the extremal
MS surface \(Y_{2,2}\), treated in `CONN-001`. The local MS-surface manuscript
constructs rank-two generalized cluster structures for universal families
\[
    XYZ=A(X)+B(Y)+\kappa,
\]
and identifies positive integer points with friezes.

Lam--Pylyavskyy's LP algebras remain relevant as a broader possible envelope,
but the positive separated subfamily already has a generalized cluster
framework.

## Failure Modes

- \(g(x)+h(y)+c\) may be reducible.
- The full Mordell family may not be covered by the positive separated
  generalized cluster framework.
- The LP structure may explain integrality but not Mordell's finiteness
  statements for all special quadratic rational equations.
