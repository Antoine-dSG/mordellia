# Mordell Source

## Location

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, p. 293.
- Main LEC tag: `LEC-183`.
- Related LEC tags: `LEC-117`, `LEC-118`.

## Atom

Mordell considers
\[
    g(x)+h(y)+c=zxy,
\]
where \(g,h\) have no constant term. For coprime \(x,y\), he identifies the
integrality condition with
\[
    g(x)+h(y)+c\equiv0\pmod{xy}.
\]

## Normal Forms

- Exchange form:
  \[
      z=\frac{g(x)+h(y)+c}{xy}.
  \]
- Quadratic specialization:
  \[
      x^2+y^2+1=xyz.
  \]
- Shifted quadratic specialization:
  \[
      x^2+y^2\pm x\pm y+1=xyz.
  \]
