# Structure

The common structure is a quartic surface together with auxiliary quadratic geometry.

For `LEC-119`, the equation
\[
    k_1FF_1=k_2G^2
\]
compares a product of two ternary quadratic forms with a square of a third. Mordell's hypotheses force a 2-adic obstruction. The decisive structure is therefore local: coefficient congruences and determinant parity prevent the existence of a nonzero integral point.

For `LEC-122`, the smooth diagonal surface
\[
    a_1x_1^4+a_2x_2^4+a_3x_3^4+a_4x_4^4=0
\]
is a K3 surface. When \(a_1a_2a_3a_4\) is a square and a suitable rational point is known, the geometry of lines and tangent constructions gives new rational points. Later work gives stronger density statements under comparable hypotheses.

Structure ranks:

- `LEC-119`: rank 3. The local obstruction is visibly related to the equation and determines Mordell's non-solubility statement.
- `LEC-122`: rank 3. The K3/diagonal-quartic structure and line configuration directly explain the point-generation statement.
