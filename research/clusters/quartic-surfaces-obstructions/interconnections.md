# Interconnections

`LEC-119` and `LEC-122` are not the same family, but they share the arithmetic pattern "quartic surface plus auxiliary quadratic geometry." In one case this geometry proves impossibility; in the other it propagates rational points.

The larger connection is with the quartic-genus-one cluster. Binary quartic equations give genus-one curves; diagonal quartic surfaces can contain genus-one fibrations. Thus the quartic surface entries should be linked to `LEC-129`, `LEC-130`, `LEC-135`, `LEC-136`, `LEC-140`, and `LEC-145`, but not merged with them: the dimension and the qualitative questions differ.
