# Interconnections

`LEC-178` is the elementary Gaussian norm case.

`LEC-179` is a general finiteness theorem for a norm form equal to a lower-degree polynomial.

`LEC-180` is the constant right-hand-side norm equation.

Outgoing links:

- Mordell/elliptic cluster: descent on elliptic curves often produces norm equations in quadratic or cubic fields.
- Quartic genus-one cluster: Pell-unit equations are norm equations in quadratic fields.
- Binary forms/Thue cluster: \(N(x+y\theta)=m\) is a binary form equation.

Interconnection rank is `2`: the entries are visibly different, but they are part of the same norm-form/unit-rank mechanism.
