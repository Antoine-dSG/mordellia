# Interconnections

`LEC-135` is the general binary quartic genus-one model.

`LEC-136` is the even quartic subfamily.

`LEC-140` is the Pell-type subfamily \(y^2=Dx^4\pm1\).

`LEC-129` and `LEC-130` are quartic Pell/Nagell--Ljunggren uniqueness phenomena.

`LEC-145` connects ternary quartic equations to binary quartic curves and genus-one finiteness.

Outgoing links:

- Mordell/elliptic cluster: a rational point turns a genus-one quartic into an elliptic curve.
- Binary forms/Thue cluster: integral points often reduce to Thue equations.
- Norm equations: Pell-unit arguments are norm equations in quadratic fields.

Interconnection rank is `2`: Mordell presents several different-looking quartic equations, but they belong to a larger family of binary quartic/genus-one/Pell-unit structures.
