# Interconnections

`LEC-162` is the general finiteness theorem.

`LEC-050` is the binary cubic case and is also the mechanism behind many Mordell-curve integral-point computations.

`LEC-037`, `LEC-038`, and `LEC-051` are local cubic-form obstructions. They should be connected to any later equation whose proof reduces to a binary cubic congruence.

Important outgoing links:

- Mordell/elliptic cluster: integral points on \(y^2=x^3+k\) reduce to binary cubic forms and Thue equations.
- Quartic genus-one cluster: binary quartics use a parallel invariant/reduction theory, and integer points can reduce to Thue equations.
- Norm-equation cluster: binary forms and norm forms meet when a form is realized as a field norm \(N(x+y\theta)\).

Interconnection rank is `2`: equations presented in separate parts of the book are instances of the larger binary-form/Thue mechanism.
