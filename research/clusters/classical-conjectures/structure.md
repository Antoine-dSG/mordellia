# Structure

`LEC-188` is the integral-point problem on a singular log K3 surface whose
projective closure is Cayley's four-nodal cubic. Mordell's two
prime-denominator parametrisations are universal-torsor charts. A
transcendental Brauer class unifies the quadratic-reciprocity conditions and
explains failure of strong approximation, but gives no obstruction to
existence.

`LEC-191` is structurally different. Catalan's conjecture is controlled by cyclotomic fields, primary cyclotomic units, and Galois modules. This structure is not hidden in Mordell's equation, but it is the known proof mechanism for the final theorem.

Structure ranks:

- `LEC-188`: rank 3. Known arithmetic geometry determines the form of the
  parametrisations and reciprocity restrictions, but not the truth of the
  conjecture.
- `LEC-191`: rank 4. The determining structure is known from the literature and is the proof structure of Mihailescu's theorem.
