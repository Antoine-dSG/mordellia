No strong shared arithmetic mechanism is currently recorded between `LEC-188` and `LEC-191`.

`LEC-188` connects outward to Cayley's cubic, universal torsors, log K3
surfaces, transcendental Brauer classes, Egyptian fractions, and
congruence-sieve searches. Its Brauer--Manin obstruction to strong
approximation is shared with the Markoff surface subfamily of `LEC-096`, even
though the equations do not look alike.

The only internal connection is project-level: both are concise conjectural Diophantine statements in Mordell's miscellaneous material. This is not enough for a high interconnection rank.
