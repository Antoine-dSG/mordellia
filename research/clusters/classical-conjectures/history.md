# History

`LEC-188` is the Erdos--Straus conjecture: for every integer \(n>3\),
\[
    \frac4n=\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z
\]
has a solution in positive integers. The problem is an Egyptian-fraction problem. It is enough to consider prime \(n\), and large computational verification is known, but the conjecture remains open.

`LEC-191` is Catalan's conjecture:
\[
    x^m-y^n=1
\]
has the unique solution \(3^2-2^3=1\) in integers \(x,y,m,n>1\). This is now Mihailescu's theorem. The proof uses cyclotomic fields and Galois-module methods.

History ranks:

- `LEC-188`: rank 2. It is an instance of a broad Egyptian-fraction problem, with many congruence classes and computational ranges solved, but the conjecture remains open.
- `LEC-191`: rank 1. Mordell's statement is a special case of the now-solved perfect-power gap problem, namely Catalan/Mihailescu.
