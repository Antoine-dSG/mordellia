# Markoff-Mutation Cluster

Primary entries:

- `LEC-096`: symmetric Markoff-Hurwitz surface
  \[
  x^2+y^2+z^2-axyz=b.
  \]
- `LEC-117`: asymmetric Barnes/Mordell equation
  \[
  x^2+y^2+1=xyz.
  \]
- `LEC-118`: shifted asymmetric Barnes/Mordell equations
  \[
  x^2+y^2\pm x\pm y+1=xyz.
  \]
- `LEC-183`: general divisibility/exchange family
  \[
  g(x)+h(y)+c=zxy.
  \]

Working thesis: these entries are connected by mutation. In the symmetric case, mutation is the Vieta involution in one coordinate. In the asymmetric cases, the same mechanism appears as a divisibility/exchange relation. `LEC-183` is the natural general envelope; in modern language it is closer to Laurent phenomenon algebras than to ordinary binomial cluster algebras.
