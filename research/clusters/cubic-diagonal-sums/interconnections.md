# Interconnections

`LEC-041`, `LEC-108`, and `LEC-109` are connected by cyclotomic factorization of \(x^3+y^3\) and descent over \(\mathbf Q(\rho)\). They should be cross-linked with the Mordell-elliptic cluster because \(x^3+y^3=k\) is birational to a \(j=0\) Weierstrass cubic.

`LEC-043` connects this same \(j=0\) material to the Hesse pencil. The specialization \(a=b=c=1\) is not merely similar to `LEC-041`; it is the standard normal form for a pencil of plane cubic curves with visible 3-torsion geometry.

`LEC-100` and `LEC-114` share the additive cube obstruction modulo \(9\). They should be kept in the same cluster but separated structurally from the elliptic part: their direct problem is representation by sums of cubes, not rational points on a fixed elliptic curve.

External links:

- Mordell-elliptic cluster: `LEC-060`, `LEC-066`, `LEC-084`--`LEC-092`, `LEC-190`.
- Binary-forms-Thue cluster: cubic descent and integer-point computations can reduce to binary cubic forms.
- Classical conjectures cluster: the four-cubes conjecture in `LEC-114` has the same "simple statement, weak structure" profile as `LEC-188`.
