# LEC-100

Equation:
\[
    x^3+y^3+z^3=n.
\]

Mordell records the elementary obstruction \(n\equiv\pm4\pmod9\), and also notes that there are infinitely many \(n\not\equiv\pm4\pmod9\) with no representation by positive cubes.

For integer cubes with signs allowed, the central structural gap remains: the congruence obstruction is necessary, but no complete sufficiency theorem is known. Modern work is computational and heuristic, with major progress on individual \(n\), including \(33\) and \(42\).

Interconnections: this belongs with `LEC-114` by additive cube arithmetic and with `LEC-041` only through the common cubic residue obstruction; it should not be treated as an elliptic-curve problem for fixed \(n\) without additional structure.
