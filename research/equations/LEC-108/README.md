# LEC-108

Equation:
\[
    z^2=x^3+y^3.
\]

Mordell gives a parametrisation of the integer solutions subject to \(y\) odd and \(\gcd(x,y)=1\).

The structure is the low-genus Fermat-type equation with signature \((3,3,2)\), together with the cyclotomic factorization of \(x^3+y^3\). This is a solved structural entry, not merely a search problem.

Interconnections: `LEC-108` is the solved parametrised analogue of `LEC-109`; both are close to `LEC-041` through \(x^3+y^3\), and to elliptic \(j=0\) material through the same cubic factorization.
