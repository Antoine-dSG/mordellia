# History

Mordell proves that the weighted number of nonnegative solutions is \(3G(d)\),
where \(G(d)\) counts binary quadratic forms of determinant \(-d\). In standard
discriminant notation, \(G(d)=H(4d)\), the Hurwitz class number.

Mordell records this entry on p.~291--292. The blueprint statement status is `statement`.

Later work determines when a strictly positive solution fails to exist.  The
criterion is that \(d\in\{1,4,18\}\), or that \(d\equiv2\pmod4\) is squarefree
and the class group of discriminant \(-4d\) has exponent at most \(2\).

References:

- Mordell, `Diophantine Equations`, pp. 291--292.
- Louboutin--Newman, `On the diophantine equation xy+yz+zx=d`, 1998.
- Kani, `Idoneal numbers and some generalizations`, 2011.
