# LEC-119

Equation:
\[
    k_1(ax^2+by^2+cz^2)(a_1x^2+b_1y^2+c_1z^2)
    =
    k_2(px^2+qy^2+rz^2)^2.
\]

Mordell gives a non-solubility theorem under congruence and determinant hypotheses. The statement is direct, not auxiliary: the hypotheses force no nonzero integer solutions.

The relevant structure is local. The coefficients and determinant conditions are mod \(8\) data for a quartic surface built from ternary quadratic forms. The clean modern rewrite should be a \(\mathbf Q_2\)-obstruction statement.

Interconnections: this is paired with `LEC-122` as a quartic-surface entry, but its mechanism is obstruction rather than point generation.
