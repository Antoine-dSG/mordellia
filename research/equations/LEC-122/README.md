# LEC-122

Equation:
\[
    a_1x_1^4+a_2x_2^4+a_3x_3^4+a_4x_4^4=0.
\]

Mordell's statement is a point-generation result: if \(a_1a_2a_3a_4\) is a square and one suitable rational point is known, another rational point is obtained unless the corresponding line lies on the surface.

The modern structure is the diagonal quartic K3 surface. Later work studies rational-point searches, the 48 lines, and density of rational points under hypotheses close to Mordell's square-product condition.

Interconnections: `LEC-122` is linked to `LEC-119` through quartic-surface arithmetic, and to the quartic-genus-one cluster through elliptic fibrations and quartic models.
