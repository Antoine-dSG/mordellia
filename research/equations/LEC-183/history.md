# History

Mordell's 1952 paper studies
\[
    ax^3+by^3+c\equiv0\pmod{xy}
\]
and claims broader results for \(f(x)+g(y)+c\pmod{xy}\). Schinzel completed
the proof and identified corrections in 2015.

Mordell records this entry on p.~293. The blueprint statement status is `statement`.

Kollar--Villalobos-Paz reinterpret the cubic equations as affine cubic
surfaces with infinite discrete automorphism groups.  Kollar--Li then prove
that every separated cubic with nonzero cubic leading coefficients has
infinitely many integral points.
