# LEC-043

Equation:
\[
    ax^3+by^3+cz^3+dxyz=0.
\]

The specialization \(a=b=c=1\) is a Hesse cubic. Mordell's statements concern the number of rational or integral points, with exceptional finite cases and otherwise infinitely many points.

The structural explanation is plane-cubic geometry. A nonsingular plane cubic with a rational point is an elliptic curve; the Hesse pencil makes the 3-torsion and flex geometry visible. Mordell's alternatives are therefore elliptic-curve alternatives in Hesse-type coordinates.

Interconnections: this is the natural bridge between `LEC-041` and the general elliptic-curve material. It also connects to the Markoff/mutation cluster only weakly: both have replacement transformations, but the Hesse group law is the relevant mechanism here.
