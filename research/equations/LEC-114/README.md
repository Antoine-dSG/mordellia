# LEC-114

Equation:
\[
    x^3+y^3+z^3+w^3=n.
\]

Mordell records the four-cubes conjecture: every integer is a sum of four integer cubes. He also records the known result for \(n\not\equiv\pm4\pmod9\).

The current structural situation is weak compared with the elliptic entries. Congruence classes and explicit identities prove many cases, but the remaining \(n\equiv\pm4\pmod9\) classes are not structurally resolved.

Interconnections: `LEC-114` is closest to `LEC-100`; both are additive cube representation problems and share the modulo \(9\) obstruction. It should not be merged with the elliptic two-cube entries unless a fixed-parameter geometric reformulation is being studied.
