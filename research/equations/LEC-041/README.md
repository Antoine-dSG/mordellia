# LEC-041

Equation:
\[
    x^3+y^3=az^3.
\]

Mordell's statements are non-solubility statements for specific prime-supported values of \(a\), first over \(\mathbf Q(\rho)\) and then over \(\mathbf Q\). The modern normal form is the \(j=0\) cubic twist
\[
    E_k:\ x^3+y^3=k.
\]

The relevant structure is the factorization
\[
    x^3+y^3=(x+y)(x+\rho y)(x+\rho^2y)
\]
over \(\mathbf Q(\rho)\), followed by descent. This is visibly the same structure as 3-isogeny descent on \(E_k\).

Interconnections: `LEC-041` links to `LEC-108` and `LEC-109` through the two-cube factorization, to `LEC-043` through Hesse cubic geometry, and to the Mordell-elliptic cluster through \(j=0\) elliptic curves.
